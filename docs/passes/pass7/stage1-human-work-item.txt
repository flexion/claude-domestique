**Findings**

1. `conflict` — AC1 and AC5 — AC1 says nothing is drafted until a person agrees. AC5 says resolving the last blocker produces a new draft with no one asked again. Read as a gate on each draft, the two cannot both hold.

2. `logic-problem` — AC1 — the person can be asked and decline. That branch has no outcome. Nothing says whether the criteria sit unused, whether the person can be asked again later, or whether anything is recorded.

3. `logic-problem` — AC3 — covers only refined criteria that no boundary can be built from. Drafting can also fail for reasons that are not about the criteria, or stop partway. Those cases have no outcome.

4. `untestable-criterion` — AC2 against Out of scope, second bullet — AC2 judges a draft by whether the reviewer had to write an entry themselves. Out of scope declines to fix what entries a boundary has. A reviewer who writes an entry cannot tell whether the draft missed it or whether it was never part of a boundary.

5. `missing-requirement` — Title, Description, Goal, AC3 — "boundary", "refined criteria", "freezes" and "the agent stage" are never explained. A reader who does not already work in this process cannot say what is being built or what would make it done.

6. `missing-requirement` — Problem — says why this is needed. Does not say why now. The next stage cannot invent it.

7. `missing-requirement` — Description, first sentence — "That holds for a couple of items and not for a sprint" reads as a condition about scale, and no criterion measures scale. Either it is a requirement and needs a number, or it is the reason for the work and needs none.

8. `missing-requirement` — AC1, AC2, AC7 — three actors: the person asked before drafting, the reviewer of the draft, the person whose approval is recorded. Nothing says whether these are one role or three.

9. `implementation-leak` — AC3 — names "the drafter" as a thing and "the issue" as where records go. Both are how, not what.

Seven acceptance criteria is more than one goal's worth. AC1 to AC5 are about producing a boundary from refined criteria and recording blockers when it cannot be produced.
